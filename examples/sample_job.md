We are looking for a Finance AI Product Manager to build AI-assisted workflows
for accounting, reconciliation, expense review, invoice processing, and monthly
close. The role works with finance users, engineering, IT, and data teams to
translate financial policies and operating rules into product requirements,
workflow automation, knowledge bases, prompt templates, and human review flows.

Responsibilities include:

- Identify high-frequency finance workflows suitable for AI or rules-based
  automation.
- Define PRDs, user stories, success metrics, and rollout plans.
- Partner with engineering on ERP, BI, data warehouse, and API integrations.
- Design controls for permission management, audit trails, exception handling,
  human-in-the-loop review, and output quality evaluation.
- Track automation rate, accuracy, cycle-time reduction, adoption, and risk
  reduction.

Requirements:

- Bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, information systems, business, or a
  related field.
- 2+ years of product, finance systems, finance operations, BI, or automation
  experience.
- Working knowledge of SQL, data quality checks, and finance processes such as
  AP, AR, reconciliation, budgeting, or reporting.
- Familiarity with LLMs, RAG, agents, workflow automation, or AI evaluation.
